## English

"HK 16-Tile Mahjong" (the "App") is published by Keith Lau.

**We do not collect any personal data.**

- The App is an offline, single-player game. All game data (settings,
  custom scoring tables, match progress) is stored only on your device
  and is never transmitted to any server.
- The App requires no network connection, contains no advertising, no
  in-app purchases, and uses no analytics or tracking tools.
- The App does not access your contacts, location, photos, or any
  device identifiers.
- Uninstalling the App deletes all associated data.

The App uses points for scoring only. It involves no real money, no
gambling, and no prizes of any kind.

If a future version adds online play with friends, this policy will be
revised before that update ships and announced on the store page.
