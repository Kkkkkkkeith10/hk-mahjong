唔係廣東十三張，唔係台灣麻將 — 係香港人打開嗰隻港式台牌。

● 叮 — 聽牌落叮，門叮15番，叮過水照規矩執行

● 花糊 — 八仙過海、七搶一
● 槓上開花、搶槓、海底撈月，樣樣齊

【內置規則書】

# English (United States) — secondary listing

## App name

HK 16-Tile Mahjong

## Short description

Authentic Hong Kong 16-tile mahjong (Toi Pai): Ting, ledger scoring, jokers, custom fan table.

## Full description

Authentic Hong Kong-style 16-tile mahjong (港式台牌), built to the Hong
Kong Mahjong Association rulebook. Not Cantonese 13-tile, not Taiwanese
mahjong — the genuine HK Toi Pai game.

Play against three cha-chaan-teng AI characters in a bright arcade
interface. Full rules support: Ting declarations, the la/kick-half
deferred-payment ledger, full-payment settlement, flower hands, robbing
the kong and more. Customize your table: 0/4/8 jokers, opening tile
swap, and a fully editable 153-entry scoring table with one-tap reset
to official values. Includes a built-in rulebook and fan encyclopedia.

100% offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no real money — points
scoring only. No personal data collected.

Online rooms with friends coming in a future update!
