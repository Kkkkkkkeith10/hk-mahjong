# English listing (en-US, optional secondary language)

## App name
HK Mahjong 16 Tiles

## Short description
Authentic Hong Kong 16-tile mahjong. Play offline vs AI or host private online rooms.

## Full description
Authentic Hong Kong 16-tile mahjong (港式台牌), following the official Hong Kong Mahjong Association rules — ting declarations, la-chung, jokers and flower tiles all included.

FEATURES
- Genuine HK Tai Pai rules (not the Taiwanese variant)
- Single-player vs three AI opponents, fully offline
- Private online rooms: share a 6-letter room code with friends
- Host controls: match length (4/8/16 hands), jokers, tile swap, la-chung, turn timer
- Disconnect protection: an AI takes over your seat and you can rejoin anytime
- Full official scoring table, customisable
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no sign-up

HOW TO PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Create a room, share the code, friends join, start. Empty seats are filled by AI.
